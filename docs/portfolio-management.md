# Optional portfolio notes

These decisions sit outside the workbook workflow. The workbook estimates tax on investments sold to pay bills. Use this guide to decide whether to sell an investment in a taxable brokerage account to realize a gain or loss. Review the decision when you rebalance, after a market decline or before year-end.

1. **Review losses.** A sale at a loss may offset gains or reduce taxable income. If you replace the investment, keep your intended mix and check for a wash sale, which can prevent you from claiming the loss now. Look for purchases of the same or a substantially identical investment in the 30 days before and after the sale. Include your spouse’s accounts, IRAs and automatic reinvestments. [IRS investment income guide](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550)
2. **Review gains in lower-income years.** Selling a long-held investment can make sense when the tax cost is low. Estimate the federal and California tax and any change in your Marketplace health insurance credit. Include planned Roth conversions in the same estimate. [California capital gains and losses](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/income-types/capital-gains-and-losses.html) · [Marketplace income guide](https://www.healthcare.gov/income-and-household-information/income/)
3. **Record the decision.** If you trade, record the gain or loss, the new cost basis, and any unused losses to enter from your tax return. If the benefit is small or the trade adds investment risk, keep your existing holdings.
