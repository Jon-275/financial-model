# Glossary

Plain-language definitions for retirement, tax, healthcare and workbook terms used in the guides and report.

Jump to: [Accounts and withdrawals](#accounts-and-withdrawals) · [Taxes and healthcare](#taxes-and-healthcare) · [Investments and market risk](#investments-and-market-risk) · [Workbook terms](#workbook-terms)

## Accounts and withdrawals

| Term | Plain-language meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <a id="ira"></a>**IRA** | Individual Retirement Arrangement: a tax-advantaged retirement account, either traditional (pre-tax) or Roth. |
| <a id="roth"></a>**Roth** | A retirement account funded with after-tax dollars whose qualified withdrawals are tax-free. |
| <a id="rmd"></a>**RMD** | Required Minimum Distribution: the minimum yearly withdrawal the IRS requires from pre-tax accounts starting in your 70s. |
| <a id="sepp"></a>**SEPP** | Substantially Equal Periodic Payments: IRS rule 72(t) scheduled withdrawals from pre-tax accounts before age 59.5 without early-withdrawal penalty. |
| <a id="qcd"></a>**QCD** | Qualified Charitable Distribution: a direct transfer from an IRA to charity after age 70.5 that can count toward an RMD. |
| <a id="hsa"></a>**HSA** | Health Savings Account: a tax-advantaged account paired with an eligible high-deductible health plan for medical costs. |
| <a id="fra"></a>**FRA** | Full Retirement Age: the Social Security claiming age at which you receive 100% of your primary benefit. |

## Taxes and healthcare

| Term | Plain-language meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <a id="irs"></a>**IRS** | Internal Revenue Service: the US federal tax agency that publishes annual tax brackets, standard deductions and RMD tables. |
| <a id="us"></a>**US** | United States federal tax rules, Social Security benefits and Medicare thresholds modeled in the planner. |
| <a id="ca"></a>**CA** | California state income tax and State Disability Insurance rules modeled alongside federal taxes. |
| <a id="agi"></a>**AGI** | Adjusted Gross Income: total taxable income minus specific above-the-line adjustments before itemized or standard deductions. |
| <a id="magi"></a>**MAGI** | Modified Adjusted Gross Income: AGI plus certain add-backs used to test Roth, ACA subsidy and Medicare IRMAA thresholds. |
| <a id="niit"></a>**NIIT** | Net Investment Income Tax: the 3.8% federal surtax on investment income above statutory MAGI thresholds. |
| <a id="sdi"></a>**SDI** | State Disability Insurance: California payroll tax withheld from covered wage income. |
| <a id="aca"></a>**ACA** | Affordable Care Act: federal health-insurance marketplace rules and income-based premium tax credits before Medicare eligibility at 65. |
| <a id="cobra"></a>**COBRA** | Federal continuation coverage allowing temporary retention of an employer health plan after leaving work. |
| <a id="irmaa"></a>**IRMAA** | Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount: higher Medicare Part B and Part D premiums triggered by prior-year MAGI thresholds. |

## Investments and market risk

| Term | Plain-language meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <a id="cpi"></a>**CPI** | Consumer Price Index: a measure of inflation used to adjust spending, tax brackets and real-dollar views. |
| <a id="cola"></a>**COLA** | Cost-of-Living Adjustment: an annual inflation update applied to Social Security or pension benefits. |
| <a id="tips"></a>**TIPS** | Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities: US government bonds whose principal adjusts with inflation. |
| <a id="paired-paths"></a>**Paired paths** | Two runs of the plan under the same markets: one with fixed spending and one with the spending-cut rule. |
| <a id="sequence-of-returns"></a>**Sequence of returns** | How early market losses during withdrawals can deplete a portfolio even when long-run average returns look adequate. |

## Workbook terms

| Term | Plain-language meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <a id="real-dollars"></a>**Real dollars** | Figures expressed in today's purchasing power after removing projected inflation. |
| <a id="nominal-dollars"></a>**Nominal dollars** | Future-year dollar amounts including projected inflation, matching future tax returns and account statements. |
| <a id="first-shortfall"></a>**First shortfall** | The earliest age in a projection or market path where liquid savings cannot cover planned spending and taxes. |
| <a id="vba"></a>**VBA** | Visual Basic for Applications: the built-in Excel macro language that runs the on-demand market-risk simulation on Mac. |
| <a id="pdf"></a>**PDF** | Portable Document Format: the fixed-layout six-page report exported from the Summary sheet. |
